Playing the game

How do I play?

Tap a tile to rotate its arrow one quarter-turn clockwise. The goal shown above the board tells you which direction everything needs to face. Get the whole board pointing that way and the level is solved.

What are the different modes?

  • Short Run — four quick levels. No clock, no pressure.
  • Endless Streak — solve boards until you slip. The clock tightens as you go.
  • Frozen Tile — some tiles are iced and locked. Solve their neighbours to melt them.
  • UR CRAZY — ice and a shrinking clock together. The hard one.
  • Keep Up SLOWPOKE — rows rise toward a line. Finish them before they reach it.
  • Meltdown — turn each falling piece before it hits the lava.
  • Daily — one challenge a day, the same one for everybody.

What are gems for, and how do I get them?

Gems unlock skins — tile looks, several of which add their own tap effects. You earn gems by playing: completing runs, hitting streaks, and collecting the daily login bonus, which grows each day you come back. Miss a day and the bonus streak resets. Gems cannot be bought.

My daily login streak reset even though I played

The streak rolls over at midnight in your device's local time zone, so a session just before midnight and one just after count as two different days. Changing your device's date or time zone can also affect it.

Purchases

What does "Remove Ads" include?

It's a one-time, non-consumable purchase that permanently turns off the interstitial ads that appear between rounds. Rewarded ads stay available, because those only ever play when you choose to watch one for a bonus — nothing plays without you asking for it.

I bought Remove Ads but the ads came back

Open Settings inside the game and tap Restore purchases. This re-checks your Apple ID's purchase history and reinstates it. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with. If it still doesn't restore, email us with the date of purchase and we'll help.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple, so refunds go through them, not us. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.

Progress and data

Is my progress backed up?

Progress, gems, skins and streaks are stored on your device, so deleting the app clears them. Your Remove Ads purchase is the exception — it's tied to your Apple ID and can always be restored with the button in Settings.

How do I get my leaderboard entry removed?

Email us with the display name and score you posted and we'll delete it.

Can I change my leaderboard name?

Yes — you're asked for a display name when you post a score, and you can enter a different one next time. It's never verified and doesn't have to be your real name (we'd rather it wasn't).

Ads and privacy

Why did the app ask about tracking?

That's Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and it only affects whether ads are personalized. Decline it and everything in the game still works exactly the same — the ads are just less relevant.

I'm in the EU/UK and want to change my ad consent

Open Settings in the game and tap Ad privacy → Manage. That row appears only for players in regions where the consent form applies.

Troubleshooting

  • An ad won't load. Usually a temporary fill or connection issue. The game never blocks on an ad — if one can't load, play continues without it.
  • The game feels slow or animations stutter. Try closing other apps, or restarting the device. If it persists on a specific mode or skin, tell us which — that detail helps a lot.
  • Sound or haptics aren't working. Check the toggles in the game's Settings, then your device's silent switch and Settings → Sounds & Haptics.
  • Does it work offline? Yes. The leaderboard and ads need a connection, but every mode is playable without one.
