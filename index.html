SwitchUp — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 28, 2026 · Contact: smilespergallon3019@gmail.com

SwitchUp is a puzzle game for iOS and Android. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. The short version: SwitchUp has no accounts and no sign-up, and never asks for your name, email, or contacts. It collects pseudonymous gameplay analytics, the data needed to show ads, and — only if you choose to post a score to the leaderboard — a display name you pick yourself.

Information we collect

Gameplay analytics. The app records gameplay events — for example which game mode was played, how a run ended, whether the tutorial was completed, and whether an ad was watched. These events are tagged with a randomly generated install identifier and session identifier created by the app. These identifiers are random values; they are not derived from your identity, device serial numbers, or advertising identifiers, and we cannot use them to identify you personally. Analytics data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore).

Leaderboard scores and display name. SwitchUp has an optional global leaderboard. If you submit a score, your best score and a display name you choose (up to 16 characters) are stored with Google Firebase and shown to other players alongside your rank. The name is whatever you type — it is never verified, does not need to be your real name, and we suggest you don't use your real name. Leaderboard entries are linked to a pseudonymous player identifier, not to your identity. If you never open or post to the leaderboard, nothing is stored. To have a leaderboard entry removed, contact us at the address above with the display name and score and we will delete it.

Crash and error data. If the app encounters an error, a technical description of the error (such as the error message and location in the app's code) is recorded through the same analytics pipeline to help us fix bugs.

Advertising data. SwitchUp shows ads served by Google AdMob (see "Advertising and your choices" below for the formats and controls). When an ad is requested, Google may process device information such as your IP address, device model, and — only with your permission — your device's advertising identifier.

Purchase information. SwitchUp offers a single optional in-app purchase ("Remove Ads"). Payment is handled entirely by Apple's App Store (or Google Play on Android). We never receive or store your name, billing address, or payment card details — the app only learns that the purchase was made, so it can turn interstitial ads off. That purchase state is saved on your device and can be restored through your store account with "Restore purchases".

Data stored only on your device. Your game progress, settings, unlocked cosmetics, in-game currency ("gems"), daily streaks, and Remove Ads status are saved locally on your device and are not uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Sharing your results. The optional "share" button for the Daily challenge opens your device's standard share sheet with a short text summary of your run. Choosing where it goes happens entirely in your device's share sheet — the app does not access your contacts and does not learn who you shared with.

What we do not collect: no real names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment card details. The only name the app ever stores is the leaderboard display name you choose to enter, described above.

How we use information

We use analytics data to understand how the game is played (for example, how many players return the day after installing, or which modes are most popular), to balance difficulty, and to find and fix bugs. We use leaderboard data solely to display rankings in the game. We use advertising data solely to serve the ads described below. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for any purpose beyond operating and improving the game.

Advertising and your choices

SwitchUp shows two kinds of ads:

  • Rewarded ads — always optional and opt-in. They play only when you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game benefit (for example, continuing a run).
  • Interstitial ads — short full-screen ads that occasionally appear between rounds. New players get an ad-free grace period, and the one-time "Remove Ads" purchase turns interstitials off permanently. Rewarded ads remain available after the purchase because they only ever play at your request.

Your personalization choices:

  • On iOS, the app asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any advertising identifier is used. If you decline (or later turn it off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking), ads still work — they are simply non-personalized.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland, the app shows a consent form before any ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy inside the app.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization in Settings → Google → Ads.

Ad serving is provided by Google AdMob. Google's use of advertising data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-party services

SwitchUp uses these third-party service providers:

  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) — stores the pseudonymous gameplay analytics and leaderboard entries described above. Firebase privacy documentation.
  • Google AdMob — serves the rewarded and interstitial ads described above. AdMob privacy information.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — process the optional Remove Ads purchase under their own terms and privacy policies; we never see your payment details.

These providers process data on infrastructure that may be located outside your country, including in the United States, under their own safeguards for international transfers.

Data retention and deletion

Analytics events are retained while we are actively developing and operating the game, and deleted or aggregated when no longer needed. Leaderboard entries are retained while the leaderboard operates, and deleted on request as described above. Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the app (your Remove Ads purchase remains restorable through your store account). Because analytics identifiers are random and not linked to your identity, we usually cannot tell which records belong to you; if you contact us at the address above we will help with any deletion request to the extent we can associate the data with you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and the right to complain to a data-protection authority. We honor these requests regardless of where you live — contact us at smilespergallon3019@gmail.com. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act.

Children

SwitchUp is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes — for example, new ad formats, new purchases, or new online features — we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
